# Audit: 2026-09-06 to 2026-09-11

**Status: standing record, written 2026-09-11.** Twenty-nine pull requests, #55 to #83, read
whole against the charter, the ADRs and the records; the live path exercised against paper
TWS; the suite run. This file is the audit's evidence and its correction plan. The rows it
opened live in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md), and each finding below names its row so the two
stay tied. Nothing here changes what was built; it says what was found.

The audit ran as three read-only reviews - the live and operations path, the validation and
attribution path, and record-keeping - with every claim checked in the code by the auditor
before it was ranked, and the highest one confirmed against the broker. **Confirmed** means
the code path was read end to end or the behaviour was observed; **plausible** means the
mechanism is real and the trigger was not reproduced.

## Findings

Ranked by what they cost if left. Severity 1 is a safety claim the record makes that the code
does not keep; 2 is a wrong behaviour on the VM's schedule; 3 is a defect with no trigger yet
or a misleading record; 4 is coverage.

### Severity 1

**F1. The sweep's cancel has never run since 2026-09-10.** Confirmed live. `cancel_working.sweep`
waits on `not strategy.outstanding()`, which is true before `on_start` has found any order, so
`wait_for_settlement` returns without yielding to the node task, `handle.stop()` is requested
before the node has started, and `run_async` sees the stop during startup and aborts without
starting the trader - so `cancel_all_orders` is never issued. Introduced by #66, which replaced
a fixed sleep (which did yield) with the poll. Every sweep since - the evening's last step,
`day sweep`, the close phase, `kill`'s third limb - cancelled nothing and read `BROKER CLEAR`
only because the account was already clear; the census (`census`, which waits on
`strategy.started`) is correct, which is why the verdict was honest. The unit test written for
the poll asserts the behaviour that causes it. The sweep also files no record, so the
2026-09-10 measurement in the changelog has no artifact. Row: *Make the sweep's cancel
actually run*.

**F2. The acknowledgement check crashes every scheduled phase when its transport fails.**
Confirmed. `PushoverNotifier.receipt_status` has no error handling around `urlopen`,
`json.loads` or the timestamp; `ReceiptLog.outstanding` catches a bad line but not an
unreadable file; `alerter_from_environment` raises on an empty `COPILOT_ALERT_RETRY_SECONDS`
or an expire-to-retry ratio over fifty. `day.main` calls `_check_the_halt()` first, before the
session check, the environment check and any step, outside any `try`. One outstanding CRITICAL
receipt on disk plus an unreachable Pushover ends the evening, the sweep and the morning with
a traceback, no alert, no record and no heartbeat - at exactly the moment something is wrong.
Row: *Make the acknowledgement check survive its transport*.

**F3. A CRITICAL that does not deliver is forgotten.** Confirmed. The sweep sends its CRITICAL
and prints `delivery.outcome`; no caller acts on `delivered=False`; an undelivered alert
records no receipt, so the acknowledgement check can never engage the latch for it; and `day`
marks the sweep `alerts_itself`, so it raises no WARNING of its own. On a Pushover outage with
an order still working, exit 1 in the journal is the only trace and the morning's heartbeat
reads healthy. Row: *Escalate a CRITICAL that did not deliver*.

### Severity 2

**F4. The unacknowledged-CRITICAL consumer runs three times a day.** Confirmed. Only `day`
phases settle receipts (08:30, 10:30, 17:00 Eastern), against ADR-0023's one-hour deadline; a
CRITICAL from the 10:30 sweep expiring at 11:30 engages the latch at 17:00, after the 11:35
midday phase has placed orders. Stopping the `copilot-*` timers, which the runbook offered as
*pause the schedule, not trading*, also stops this consumer. Row: *Give the acknowledgement
check its own timer*.

**F5. A daily emergency-priority self-test.** Confirmed. The pre-open phase sends
`alerting --send-test --severity critical`, and #83 put the pre-open phase on a weekday timer
at 07:35 Eastern. Pushover priority 2 retries every two minutes until acknowledged. It does
not also engage the latch only because `_send_test` builds its alerter without a receipt log.
Row: *Take the CRITICAL self-test off the daily timer*.

**F6. The 10:30 sweep can overlap the 10:45 order window.** Confirmed by arithmetic. The
census retries are 600 s of waits plus up to four 90 s connection deadlines, reached only
when something is working; the order-window phase rests a probe order at 10:45. The sweep's
last census would report the probe's order `STILL WORKING`, CRITICAL, exit 1. Nothing
serialises the two units. Row: *Serialise the 10:30 sweep and the 10:45 order window*.

**F7. `kill` does not stop a running session.** Confirmed. The latch is read once, at build;
a node already up keeps an `ACTIVE` engine until it exits, and the only immediate limb is the
cancel (F1). ADR-0027 documents the build-time read; the runbook advertises *stop all trading
on this host*. Row: *Re-read the latch inside a running node*.

**F8. The evening will cancel its own basket the day orders are enabled.** Confirmed, latent.
`day`'s docstring says the sweep moves to 10:30 then; the 10:30 timer was added and the
evening's sweep step stayed. Inert while `run_activation` hardcodes orders denied. Row: *Make
the evening's sweep conditional on orders denied*.

**F9. Runbook claims the code contradicts** - corrected in #84. An engaged latch does not make
`day` *nothing to do* (it prints and runs, orders denied); committing the Gateway digest breaks
a test that asserts the committed compose is unpinned; the kill drill chained its release on
the sweep's exit code, which is non-zero whenever the census is not clear; the stage-four pass
condition omitted Docker, the clock and the catalog path.

### Severity 3, operations

**F10. The flood guard is inert on the VM.** Confirmed. Per-process state, oneshot units; the
*halt latch still engaged* WARNING fires from every phase. Row: *Make the flood guard span
processes*.

**F11. Day units have no `OnFailure=` and no ordering on Docker or the Gateway.** Plausible
triggers, confirmed shape. A traceback, an OOM kill at `MemoryMax=6G`, or the 45-minute
`TimeoutStartSec` on a slow vendor day notifies nobody; a reboot at 08:25 hands the 08:30
evening a Gateway still logging in. Row: *Give the day units `OnFailure=` and an ordering*.

**F12. Client-id pairs collide across modules.** Confirmed. `run_activation` and
`failure_injection` share 871/872; `order_types` and `strand_recovery`'s second session share
841/842. Separated only by the timers. Row: *Partition client ids per module*.

**F13. No locking on the latch, the receipts log or `live/out/`.** Confirmed, low impact.
Row: *Lock the latch and the receipts log*.

**F14. The latch depends on a rule no test holds.** ADR-0027's own consequence: a future
builder that adds an execution client bypasses the switch. Row: *Assert every order-capable
node is built by `build_paper_node`*.

**F15. The guard is wired into no node**, so `kill`'s recovery checklist item six names a
ledger nothing writes. Already a row (*Wire the protection guard into the basket*); noted.

### Severity 3, research

The three claims the week rests on hold on the filed numbers and reproduce exactly from the
filed rows: no positive four-factor alpha in any of thirteen records under either exit
treatment (largest point estimate +0.001 R); the pooled interval [-0.0176, +0.0788] does not
clear zero; the AAPL holdout is +0.035 R at 0.34 standard errors. The pooled interval, block
length, concurrency and seed sequence were re-derived from the record and match to six
places. What follows is where the record oversells or under-describes them.

**F16. ADR-0024's predeclared effect size has never been set.** Confirmed. Every activation
files `minimum_effect_r: ""`, mapped to zero; the holdout gate is `lower_r > 0`; the ADR's
anti-tuning rule guards nothing. Row: *Set the predeclared effect size before the next
holdout*.

**F17. `effective_trades` equals the raw count on nine of twelve verdicts.** Confirmed.
Concurrency is exactly one for a one-position strategy, and the autocorrelation time is
measured in trade order, where R has no lag-one dependence; the module was written for
calendar clustering. Re-bootstrapped at block lengths 20, 40, 80 and by calendar year: no
verdict moves. Row: *Count effective trades on the calendar, not the trade index*.

**F18. The attribution fit is unweighted over a leverage of 5 to 380, on a bracketed payoff.**
Confirmed. SPY next-close's market loading reads 0.79 with the exit session and 1.40 without,
for a long-SPY premise; the top percentile of trades carries about twenty-five times a median
trade. R is capped above by the target and open below (minimum -4.2 R), so the linear model
is misspecified in the direction that attenuates beta and flatters alpha. The no-alpha verdict
is therefore stronger than ADR-0026 argues. Row: *Weight or scale the attribution regression*.

**F19. The bracket's stated direction is contradicted by its own output.** Confirmed. AAPL
next-close: -0.026 included, +0.001 excluded. `bracketed_verdict` is an agreement test. Row:
*State the exit-session bracket as an agreement test*.

**F20. The AAPL holdout's reassessment block was written by hand**, carries fields
`Evidence.as_record()` does not emit, and the record has no `trade_rows`; `attribute` never
reads it. Row: *Regenerate the AAPL holdout reassessment through code*.

**F21. ADR-0024 and `pool.py` still quote the gross interval.** Confirmed; the correction lives
only in the changelog. Row: *Supersede ADR-0024's worked example*.

**F22. The pool's headline and its interval are different quantities, unlabelled.** Confirmed:
`mean_oos_net_r` 0.060 is a mean of fold scores; `evidence.mean_r` 0.030 is per trade. Row:
*Label the pool's two means*.

**F23. The pool's membership changes at both ends inside one verdict**, and `majority: true`
sits beside `clears_zero: false`. Confirmed. Row: *Hold the pool's membership constant at
both ends*.

**F24. Thirteen attributions are one body of evidence read thirteen times.** Confirmed. Row:
*Say the thirteen attributions are not thirteen tests*.

**F25. `MIN_TRADES_FOR_ATTRIBUTION = 30` is asserted, undocumented, and a degenerate resample
aborts the run.** Confirmed, not triggered (smallest filed n is 94). Row: *Document the
attribution floor and survive a degenerate resample*.

**F26. Money passes through `as_double`** in `nautilus_replay.py` and `risk/guard.py`, where
`Money.as_decimal()` exists; `peak_qty.as_double()` likewise; `risk_amount` descends from a
float ATR. Row: *Take money out of `as_double`*.

**F27. A position open at a fold boundary is never scored.** Confirmed. Row: *Score the trade
open at a fold boundary*.

**F28. `signal_created_at` is the fill.** Confirmed. Row: *`signal_created_at` is the fill,
not the signal*.

**F29. Six small defects**, each confirmed, none triggered: a one-session-hold premise traces
back in `attribute`; a same-session round trip is misdiagnosed as a stale vintage; an interval
over fewer than two trades is malformed; an interior missing factor session is dropped
silently; `exposures` is hardwired to four factors; the R denominator is refused at zero in
one place and counted in three. Row: *Small defects in attribution and evidence*.

## Checked and sound

Recorded so the next audit does not re-derive them.

- No path submits an order while the latch is engaged: every session with orders enabled is
  built by `build_paper_node`, `apply_order_switch` only ever sets `HALTED`, and the one
  exemption is the cancels-only sweep. An unreadable latch is engaged; the first reason stands.
- An unread census is not a clear broker: the census requires the account in the cache, an
  unread final census does not inherit an earlier reading, and `UNCONFIRMED` has its own exit
  code.
- The calendar and timezone logic: everything keys off `EASTERN` as instants, early closes cut
  the close and skip the close phase, `monitoring_session` names today, `Persistent=` matches
  each phase's refusal rule, holidays are rule-based with no expiry.
- `Alerter.send` never raises; `StreamNotifier` reports `delivered=False` rather than faking
  success. `OutcomeLedger` refuses a torn line and fsyncs; the guard never releases a halt it
  did not set.
- Compose binds both ports to localhost, takes the password as a file secret, keeps settings in
  a volume, and sets `READ_ONLY_API` off; the eight timers, `host_check.TIMERS` and the
  runbook's table agree exactly; `install-units.sh` needs no root.
- The pooled interval, block length, concurrency and seed sequence reproduce from the filed
  rows alone; `net_r` reproduces every score; `require_describes` is a real check where it is
  wired; the prefix-sum bootstrap resamples what a refit would; the decomposition identity
  holds to 1e-6 across all fifty-two filed fits, and costs land in alpha as claimed.
- The factor pin is real, both digests verified on every read, units and sentinels handled;
  UTC date equals session date for every filed stamp; no holdout bar reaches the pool's
  spine, and the pooled objective selects on the train slice and reports the disjoint test
  slice.
- The cost model is `Decimal` throughout, the plan is pinned by name and recorded per verdict.
- The delta register matches the diff (36 files, 8 forecast conflicts); every ADR is present,
  indexed and unedited after acceptance except one whitespace re-pad; the roadmap's counts
  were the arithmetic sum of its groups; #74's pruning matched the retention rule; nothing
  tracked contains a token, a password or a home path.
